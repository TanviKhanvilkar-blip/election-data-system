Data sources. Candidate-level results, margins, and demographic detail (gender, age, education, criminal cases, assets): candidate affidavits filed with the Election Commission of India, compiled by the Association for Democratic Reforms. State-wise elector turnout and its actual male/female vote split: Election Commission of India.
One estimate, disclosed. Per-constituency turnout totals and vote counts are exact. The male/female split within a constituency's turnout is modeled from that state's real vote-gender ratio combined with the commonly cited national elector split (52% male / 48% female), since the Commission does not publish elector gender at constituency level.
